## Priorities and representation

1. High priority: smooth tall round cakes, square cakes, 1–4 tier proportions, board, custom colours. Existing primitives extended with constrained sizes, taper and offset.
2. High priority: rose/blossom/leaf clusters, cascade presets, pearls and borders, gold flecks, ribbons/bows, drips, plaques and numbers. Reusable procedural geometry and instanced beads. Flowers are decorative; edibility is never inferred.
3. High priority: grooves, ridges, marble, ombré, watercolour and quilting. Procedural surface textures plus geometry; all approximations shown as such.
4. Medium priority: rectangular book/gift structures, geometric ornaments, print placement markers. A rectangle cannot recreate a sculpted book, cupcake, handbag or castle. Prints remain reference-only markers without fabricated licensed artwork.
5. Reference only: characters, cosmetics, tiny handwriting, jewels, geodes/crystals, sculptural sails/fans, natural fruit/grass, detailed crowns and branded replicas. Original photographs retained for bakery review. No external models or generated replacements.

## Accuracy and confirmation

Recreated in 3D: supported basic structure, colours, board and attachment regions. Approximate visual representation: handmade surfaces, procedural flowers, metallic flecks and generic ornament shapes. Requires bakery confirmation: all advanced changes, four tiers, mixed/sculpted structures, specific topper/print artwork, flower suitability and final price. No numerical fidelity score.

Template definitions live independently in src/data/cakeTemplates.ts. All 43 IDs map explicitly; WA0020 remains gallery-only on cake-wa-0014. Photographs do not prove frosting composition or flower edibility. No approved decoration surcharges are inferred.
