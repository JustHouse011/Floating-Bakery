# Client cake photographs

The 44 `IMG-20261013-WA*.jpg` cake photographs were supplied by the client in this workspace for Floating Bakery & Caterers. No stock or generated cake imagery is used in this collection. Visible client logos, watermarks and brand details remain in the derivatives. Third-party character and fashion branding visible in supplied designs is part of the original photographs; publication rights remain for the bakery to confirm.

Original files are preserved without renaming or modification. `CAKE_IMAGE_INVENTORY.json` records original SHA-256 hashes, dimensions, byte sizes, product associations and derivative dimensions. The catalogue directory also contains 12 earlier menu/logo WebPs, giving 56 supported image files in total rather than 44; only the 44 named WhatsApp JPEGs comprise this phase's cake collection.

`scripts/qa-cake-inventory.mjs` reproducibly generates full-resolution WebP details and aspect-preserving 400-pixel-wide thumbnails under `/images/cakes/client/`. Detail derivatives do not upscale or infer cake dimensions. The mobile-gallery screenshot WA0017 is cropped to its unambiguous photograph bounds; the small visual-search icon within the photograph is retained. WA0044 loses only its black letterboxing. All other photographs retain their complete image bounds.

WA0014 and WA0020 have different file hashes but visibly show the same gold-sculpture cake design. They share one product record, with both sources represented in its cover/gallery and inventory. No exact hash duplicates were found. Other visually similar themes remain separate designs.

Descriptive catalogue names and category assignments come from visual inspection and await bakery approval. Photograph styling does not establish ingredients, cake dimensions, serving counts or exact recreation guarantees.
